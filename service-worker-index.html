About response times. Each figure is a full HTTPS request (DNS lookup, connection, encryption handshake, then the reply) sent by a monitoring probe located near the server. It is not a ping and it is not measured from your device. A full request takes several times longer than a ping, so these figures are higher than the latency shown in the FuzeVPN app and do not reflect your connection speed.
